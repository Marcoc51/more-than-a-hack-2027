MAF FC · UI3A · design direction for owner review

One product, five experiences

A single recommended direction for the MAF FC Possession Explorer: calmer surfaces, a clear three-level hierarchy, restrained line icons, progressive disclosure for secondary analytics, and a Home and Team page. Everything below is static, design-only markup.

Illustrative synthetic data. Strips, labels and metrics are copied from the S1 and S4 synthetic exports at commit 3d1b2e6. Team members are placeholders. No competition logos are used: the organisers' names appear as text, as verified in the official repository.

How the Phase 3B engine turns tracking and Smart Tagging into one label per frame, and what we can and cannot claim about it.

Control evidence

A team has credible control when its nearest player is within 2.0 m of the ball and the other team's nearest is at least 1.0 m further away. Being nearest is never enough.

home if d_home ≤ 2.0 m and d_away − d_home ≥ 1.0 m
away if d_away ≤ 2.0 m and d_home − d_away ≥ 1.0 m
else no credible control · no ball if untracked

Possession changes

A switch needs 10 control observations for the new team with no contradicting tracked frame; short dropouts pause the count. The change is backdated to the first observation.

Implementation details
Untracked frames pause the count; a dropout of 10 frames or more resets it. A run spans at least 0.4 s. Possession is held through ball dropouts of up to 250 frames (10 s).

Dead ball and restarts

Inside a set-piece tag window, the frames before the strike are dead. The strike is the first tracked frame at 4 m/s or more after at least 5 slower frames, and it must lie inside the half.
